FAQ

Common questions, straight answers.

A few things businesses usually ask before working with KC Network Tech.

How much does IT support cost in Kansas City?

It depends on your business size and needs, so I don't quote a one-size-fits-all number. Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute assessment where I learn about your setup and give you a clear, straightforward quote — no pressure, no obligation.

Do I need a monthly plan, or can I hire you for a one-time project?

Both work. Monthly retainers make sense if your team needs ongoing support, but plenty of clients hire me for one-time projects — a new office network, a Microsoft 365 migration, or a security assessment.

What size businesses do you work with?

I specialize in small and mid-sized businesses from 1 to 25 employees — companies big enough to depend on their technology, but not big enough to justify a full-time IT hire. If you're larger than that, reach out anyway and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.

Do you provide on-site support or only remote?

Both. Most day-to-day issues are resolved remotely, which keeps response times fast and costs down. For hardware, network installs, and anything that needs hands on equipment, I come on-site anywhere in the Kansas City metro. The Professional and Business plans include scheduled on-site visits every month.

Can you manage our Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Yes — user accounts, email, licensing, security policies, and migrations for both platforms are core parts of my managed IT service. If you're moving from one platform to the other (or off an old email server), I handle the migration end to end.

What happens in the free IT assessment?

We spend 30 minutes talking through your business, your current setup, and your biggest pain points. You walk away with a clear picture of where your technology stands and what I'd recommend — whether or not you hire me. No pressure, no obligation.
